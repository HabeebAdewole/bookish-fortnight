# FORM redesign discussion — 2026-09-23

## User direction

The user dislikes the current landing-page appearance and wants to take inspiration from https://cardtonic.com/upskill, including its style and animations, without copying the work. Pause dedicated-page work and settle the new landing-page direction first.

## Observed reference patterns

Inspected the live page at desktop and mobile widths, plus DOM styles at several scroll positions.

- Oversized irregular display type, clean supporting text, saturated accents, dark and light section changes.
- Illustrated hero in a sticky section. Scrolling scales the illustration up while reducing the title scale.
- Rounded horizontal cards with category colours and detailed content.
- Curved section boundaries and decorative illustrations.
- Timeline ornaments translate into place as scrolling progresses.
- Persistent header and a compact mobile presentation.

These are observed patterns, not a claim about their underlying animation library. No source code, illustration, font file, or logo has been imported from the reference.

## Proposed FORM translation — pending design discussion

Retain FORM and its wordmark. Explore a more expressive gym-club identity with ink charcoal, warm white, brighter orange, and a restrained cool accent. The exact new palette has not been agreed.

Use a distinct athletic display face and an original illustrated fitness scene: adult people moving, training equipment, and forms inspired by weight plates or running-track curves. Replace the current full-width photographic opening with this illustrated hero. Keep the existing photograph available for an optional community section.

Suggested page order: animated hero; short FORM introduction; Strength / Conditioning / Mobility cards; first-session guide; trainers; memberships; closing call to action.

Suggested interactions:
1. A short scroll-linked hero sequence with modest depth and scale changes.
2. Horizontally browsable class cards with touch, keyboard, and visible button controls.
3. A three-step first-session guide: choose a class, meet your coach, train at your pace. Explain the intended experience without claiming booking is currently available.
4. Focus and hover feedback on actionable controls; small reveals where they support hierarchy.

Use original shapes, illustrations, copy, and timing. Do not reproduce Cardtonic's character, hanging-card cable motif, exact section composition, colours as a set, or distinctive lettering. Provide a static reduced-motion variant and simplify scroll effects on mobile. Keep the schedule usable and preserve existing keyboard accessibility.

## Next step

Agree whether FORM should retain the current palette or adopt a brighter palette with the new illustrated direction. Then write the bounded redesign implementation plan. Application code has not been changed during this review.
